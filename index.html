🔥 Fire Emergency

✅ What to do

  1. Stay calm and listen carefully to your teacher.
  2. Leave using the school's assigned evacuation route.
  3. Walk calmly and do not push others.
  4. Stay with your class or group.
  5. Go to the designated assembly point.
  6. Wait for instructions from teachers or emergency personnel.

❌ What NOT to do

  • Do not use an elevator during an evacuation.
  • Do not go back into the building.
  • Do not hide instead of following the evacuation plan.
  • Do not panic or push other students.

💨 If there is smoke

Move away from smoke and follow the school's evacuation instructions. Stay as low as practical if smoke is present and get to a safe area. Tell a teacher or responsible adult immediately.

🩹 Injury & First Aid

✅ If someone is injured

  1. Tell a teacher or responsible adult immediately.
  2. Keep the injured person calm.
  3. Ask other students to give the person space.
  4. Do not move the person unless there is immediate danger.
  5. Let a trained adult provide first aid.
  6. Follow the school's emergency instructions.

❌ Remember

  • Do not try unfamiliar first-aid procedures.
  • Do not give someone medicine unless an appropriate adult or medical professional directs it.
  • Do not crowd around the injured person.

📢 The most important step

Get a teacher, school nurse, or another responsible adult as quickly as possible.

🌎 Earthquake

✅ During the shaking

  1. Stay calm.
  2. Drop down, cover your head and neck, and hold on.
  3. Stay away from windows and objects that could fall.
  4. If you are indoors, remain indoors until the shaking stops and instructions are given.
  5. Listen to your teacher.

🔎 After the shaking

  1. Check yourself for injuries.
  2. Tell a teacher if you or someone else needs help.
  3. Expect possible aftershocks.
  4. Follow the school's evacuation or safety instructions.
  5. Stay with your class.

❌ Avoid

  • Do not run toward windows.
  • Do not use elevators.
  • Do not leave the school area without permission.

🚪 Evacuation

✅ Evacuation steps

  1. Stop what you are doing and listen for instructions.
  2. Follow your teacher and the assigned evacuation route.
  3. Walk calmly. Do not push or run.
  4. Stay with your class.
  5. Go directly to the assembly point.
  6. Stay there until your teacher gives further instructions.

📋 At the assembly point

Stay with your class so teachers can check that everyone is accounted for. Do not leave the assembly area unless a responsible adult tells you to.
